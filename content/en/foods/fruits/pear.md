# Pear

## Estimated GI

Approximately 38 ± 2 (Low GI, GI ≤ 55)

## Nutritional Highlights

- Rich in dietary fiber, especially pectin, which helps slow blood sugar absorption
- High water content (over 80%)
- Good source of vitamin C and potassium
- Low in calories, about 57 kcal per 100 g

## Serving Suggestions

- **Eat with the skin on** for maximum dietary fiber
- A healthy snack between meals
- Pair with a small handful of nuts to further balance blood sugar
